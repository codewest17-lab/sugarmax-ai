Data Deletion Request

You can request full deletion of your SugarMax AI account and all associated data — profile, scans, meal images, and payment history — at any time.

What gets deleted

Your profile, subscription record, all meal scans and images, and usage logs are permanently removed. Payment records required for tax and fraud-prevention compliance may be retained in anonymized form for the period required by law.

How to request deletion

Email us from the address on your account via our contact page with the subject "Data Deletion Request." We'll confirm your identity and complete the deletion within 30 days.
