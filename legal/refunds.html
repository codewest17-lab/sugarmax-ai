Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Monthly subscriptions

SugarMax Pro is billed monthly. If you cancel, you'll keep Pro access through the end of your current billing period — we don't issue prorated refunds for partial months.

Failed or duplicate charges

If you're charged in error — a duplicate payment or a charge that didn't activate Pro — contact support with your payment reference and we'll investigate and refund confirmed errors within 5–10 business days.

How to request a refund

Reach out through our contact page with your account email and the Paystack reference from your receipt.
