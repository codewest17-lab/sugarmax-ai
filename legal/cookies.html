Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we use cookies for

SugarMax AI uses a small number of cookies and browser storage entries to keep you signed in between visits and remember your session. We don't use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Essential cookies

Authentication cookies set by Supabase are required for the app to function — they let us recognize you're logged in without asking for your password on every page.

Payment cookies

When you check out with Paystack, Paystack's checkout page may set its own cookies to process your payment securely. These are governed by Paystack's own privacy policy.

Managing cookies

You can clear cookies through your browser settings at any time, though doing so will sign you out of SugarMax AI.
