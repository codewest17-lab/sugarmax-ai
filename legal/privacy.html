Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

When you create a SugarMax AI account, we collect your name, email address, and authentication details. When you use the meal scanner, we store the photo you upload, the AI-generated nutrition analysis, and your scan history. If you upgrade to Pro, our payment processor Paystack handles your card details directly — we never see or store your full card number.

How we use it

We use your data to run the scanning feature, maintain your scan history, manage your subscription, and improve the accuracy of our AI analysis. Meal photos are sent to Google's Gemini API for analysis and are not used to train models without your consent.

Storage and security

Your data is stored in a Supabase-managed database with row-level security, meaning only you (and authorized SugarMax AI staff, where legally necessary) can access your records. Meal photos are stored in a private storage bucket accessible only to your account.

Sharing

We don't sell your personal data. We share the minimum necessary data with service providers — Google (AI analysis), Paystack (payments), and Supabase (infrastructure) — solely to operate the app.

Your rights

You can access, correct, or delete your data at any time from Settings, or by submitting a data deletion request.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out via our contact page.
